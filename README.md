# 🧅 ONION DETECT AI

### Automated Onion Quality Assessment & Grading System

ONION DETECT AI is a smart computer-vision based prototype designed to automate onion quality assessment and grading.

The system aims to reduce subjective manual inspection, improve grading consistency, reduce disputes between farmers, traders and procurement centers, and provide a digital quality record for every inspected batch.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Onion quality assessment is often performed manually, making the process subjective and inconsistent across procurement centers.

This can result in:

- Inconsistent grading
- Manual inspection errors
- Disputes between farmers and buyers
- Increased inspection time
- Difficulty maintaining quality records

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution

ONION DETECT AI provides a smartphone-based onion inspection workflow.

The user places approximately 20–50 onions on a tray and captures an image.

The prototype then analyzes the sample and provides:

- Individual onion detection
- Onion size estimation
- Defect identification
- Quality score
- Grade classification
- Batch summary
- Digital quality slip

---

## ⚙️ Main Features

### 📸 Smart Inspection
Capture or upload an image of onions placed on a tray.

### 🔍 Onion Detection
Detect and isolate individual onions from the tray image.

### 🧪 Defect Analysis
Identify visible quality issues such as:

- Rot
- Sprouting
- Surface damage
- Mold-like defects

### 📊 Automated Grading

The prototype classifies onions into:

- Grade A
- Grade B
- Reject

### 📄 Digital Quality Slip

Generate a digital inspection record containing:

- Batch ID
- Inspection date
- Total onions
- Grade distribution
- Defect
